The first release of **Shuttle**, a private assistant for GrapheneOS and other Android phones without Google services. Everything runs on your phone: no account, no API key, no root and no Shizuku.

### Highlights

- **Your assistant:** set Shuttle as the digital assistant app and your assist gesture opens a small panel at the bottom of the screen. From the launcher you get a full-screen chat with search, saved chats and settings.
- **Phone controls:** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, flashlight, Do Not Disturb, ringer, brightness, volume, media, alarms and timers, calls and texts (always confirmed first), apps and directions. Ask whether something is on, or say several commands at once.
- **Phone control (optional):** an accessibility service that taps Quick Settings tiles for the switches Android won't let apps change, or any tile by name ("battery saver on"), and takes screenshots, locks the phone, goes home or back, and opens recents or the power menu.
- **On-device model:** download Granite 4.2 3B once (about 2.1 GB) and ask anything: explanations, recipes, advice, writing, maths. Answers stream in, follow-ups use the chat so far, and lists and bold text show as formatting.
- **Web search:** for anything current, the model searches DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia and answers with tappable sources. Only the search words are sent.
- **Reminders and calendar:** "remind me to call Mom at 6", "what's on Friday?", "add dentist to my calendar Friday at 3".
- **Messages:** with notification access, "read my messages", "what did Sam say?" and "reply to Sam saying on my way", sent through the app the message came from after you confirm.
- **Weather** from Open-Meteo (no key), for where you are or any place.
- **More:** settings pages and App info by name, camera (photo, selfie, video), contact details, starting an email, notes to your notes app, and instant sums, unit conversions and world clock.
- **Voice:** speak and hear replies through your phone's own speech recognizer and text-to-speech (Distillery or any other).

### Installing

- Download **Shuttle-v0.1.0.apk** below. It installs as `app.shuttle.assistant`.
- On GrapheneOS, turn on Shuttle's **Network** permission for the model download, web search and weather.
- Phone control and notification access are optional. If Android says the setting is restricted, open Shuttle's App info, tap ⋮ and choose **Allow restricted settings** first.
- Test builds (**Shuttle Debug**, `app.shuttle.assistant.debug`) are a separate app with their own model and chats.

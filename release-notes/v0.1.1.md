Shuttle now searches the web on its own when a question needs it, instead of asking you to.

- **Current questions are searched first:** news, scores, prices, who holds an office, new releases or a recent year get a quick search before the answer, with sources underneath.
- **No more "I don't have real-time information":** if the model answers like that, or offers to search, Shuttle searches and answers again straight away.
- **"Search it" works:** "search it", "yes, look it up" or just "search" look up the question you asked before.

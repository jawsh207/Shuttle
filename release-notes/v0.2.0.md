A big step for **Shuttle**: music, routines, memory, call history, sharing into Shuttle, new weather sources, and copy and read-aloud buttons on every answer.

### New

- **Play music by name:** "play Radiohead", "play the album OK Computer", "play my running playlist on Spotify", "play some jazz". Your music player finds and plays it.
- **Routines:** one phrase runs several commands. Set them up in Settings › Routines ("good night" → Do Not Disturb on, brightness 10%, alarm at 7).
- **Memory:** "remember my wife's name is Sam", then "text my wife saying I'm on my way". Facts stay on your phone and are used in answers; review or delete them in Settings › Memory.
- **Sharing into Shuttle:** Share › Ask Shuttle from any app. Text or a link waits in the chat with Summarize, Key points, Explain and Translate, or ask your own question about it.
- **Call history:** "who called?", "any missed calls?", "call them back" (confirmed first).
- **Alarms, timers and reminders:** "when is my next alarm?", "cancel my 7am alarm", "how long is left on my timer?", "what reminders do I have?", "cancel the reminder to call Mom".
- **Copy and read aloud:** buttons on the right under every answer.

### Better

- **Weather** now comes from the US National Weather Service (for US places) and MET Norway (everywhere else), both free for any use and credited under each answer. Places are found on your phone in a list of about 170,000 towns, so only coordinates are sent.
- **Follow-ups:** "turn it off", "it's still on" and "try again" act on your last command, and Shuttle never claims to have changed something it didn't.

### Notes

- The first answer after updating is slower while the model reads its updated instructions.
- New permissions are asked for only when first needed: call history, and (as before) calendar, location and notifications.
